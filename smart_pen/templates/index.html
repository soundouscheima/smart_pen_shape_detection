How to Use:

  1. Connect MPU device to this computer
  2. Run MPU client to send data to port 5000
  3. Select shape type and quality
  4. Click "Start Recording" to begin data collection
  5. Draw the selected shape with your hand
  6. Click "Stop Recording" when finished
  7. Go to Prediction page to analyze the shape
